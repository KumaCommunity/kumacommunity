# Privacy Policy

**Last updated**: February 11, 2026

Any such processing is governed by Cloudflare’s own privacy policies and data protection practices. The site owner does not access, control, or repurpose this data and does not sell or transfer personal information to third parties.

This site uses no cookies beyond those required for basic delivery and security handled by the hosting provider.

If hosting, infrastructure, or data practices change, this policy will be updated accordingly.
