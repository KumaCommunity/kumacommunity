# Contributing

Kuma Community welcomes feedback, corrections, and suggestions for new content.
While we aren't accepting direct code contributions or pull requests yet, there
are still several ways to help improve the project.

## Contribution Policy

The project is still in its early stages, and we're keeping development and
editorial work in-house while we establish consistent standards for content,
sources, and code. We plan to open the repository to outside contributions as
the project grows.

Our source code is publicly available on
[GitHub](https://github.com/KumaCommunity/kumacommunity). You're welcome to
clone the repository for personal use, local hosting, or offline reference.
Copying, modifying, and distributing the code is subject to the terms of the
[GNU General Public License v3.0](https://github.com/KumaCommunity/kumacommunity/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Reporting Errors

If you find an error, broken link, formatting issue, or incorrect information,
please email [contact@kumacommunity.com](mailto:contact@kumacommunity.com).

Include:

1. **Page:** A direct link to the page containing the issue.
2. **Problem:** A description of what's wrong.
3. **Correction:** Your suggested fix or correction.

For factual or technical corrections, please include a reliable source when
possible. This is especially important for legal references, technical
specifications, and historical claims.

Reports without enough information to identify or verify the problem may not be
reviewed.

## Suggesting New Content

We welcome suggestions for new articles, guides, technical references, and
updates to existing material.

Send your suggestions to
[contact@kumacommunity.com](mailto:contact@kumacommunity.com) with the following
information:

1. **Content type:** Article, guide, technical reference, correction, or other.
2. **Description:** What the topic covers, why it's useful, and how it relates
   to the project.
3. **Sources:** Links to relevant documentation, research, standards, or other
   reliable references.

For original educational material, confirm that you have authority to offer it
under [CC BY-SA 4.0](/licensing). Identify third-party elements and provide
their creators, source URLs, licenses, required credits, changes made, and any
permission evidence. Code examples retain the software license unless explicitly
licensed otherwise. Flag uncertain rights for review rather than assigning a
license. You retain copyright in your contributions.

We prioritize topics with practical civilian applications, supported by reliable
sources and consistent with applicable laws. Information that can be reused or
adapted to different situations is particularly useful.

## What We Look For

Our content focuses on clear documentation, useful skills, and approaches people
can apply to their own circumstances. We prefer open standards, repeatable
methods, and solutions that don't depend unnecessarily on specific products or
services.

Suggestions that fit these priorities are welcome, even if they aren't currently
covered by the site.
