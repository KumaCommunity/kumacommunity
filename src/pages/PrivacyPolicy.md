# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 22, 2026

Kuma Community operates [kumacommunity.com](https://kumacommunity.com/). This
policy explains what information is processed when you visit the website or
contact us.

## Information We Process

**Website visits.** Cloudflare hosts and protects the website. To handle
requests, it may process your IP address, requested URL (including query
parameters), browser and device information, referring page, request time, and
approximate location derived from your IP address.

The site has no user accounts, public comments, or application database of
visitor information. We do not use advertising networks, marketing trackers, or
Cloudflare Web Analytics.

**Request logs.** We have disabled persistent Cloudflare Workers request logs
and configured query-string redaction for Workers observability. Cloudflare may
still process or retain traffic and security information through its separate
network and security services. We do not maintain our own archive of visitor
request logs.

**Email.** If you contact
[contact@kumacommunity.com](mailto:contact@kumacommunity.com), we receive your
email address, message, and any attachments you send. Our email is hosted by
Proton Mail.

## How Information Is Used

We use these services and the information they process to deliver the website,
address security and availability issues, and respond to messages or content
suggestions. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted
advertising.

## Cookies and Browser Storage

Cloudflare may set security cookies, including `cf_clearance` or `__cf_bm`, when
needed to check requests or respond to suspected abuse. These cookies are not
necessarily set on every visit. Details are available in
[Cloudflare's cookie documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/).

The site may store your light or dark theme preference in your browser. We do
not use cookies or browser storage for advertising or visitor profiling.

## Service Providers, Security, and Retention

**Cloudflare** provides hosting, content delivery, and security services. The
site requires HTTPS and is configured to use TLS 1.2 or later, HTTP Strict
Transport Security (HSTS), and Cloudflare's firewall and DDoS protections.
Cloudflare's handling of technical and security information is described in its
[Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

**Proton Mail** receives and stores email sent to the project under its
[privacy policy](https://proton.me/mail/privacy-policy). We may retain
correspondence to handle inquiries, review corrections or suggestions, and
maintain relevant project records. There is no fixed email deletion schedule.

These providers may process information outside your country. We may also
disclose information when required by law. Links to other websites are governed
by their own privacy policies.

## Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete
personal information. To ask about information you have provided, or request its
deletion, email [contact@kumacommunity.com](mailto:contact@kumacommunity.com).
Requests will be reviewed under applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping
requirements.

The website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children
under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

## Policy Changes

We may update this policy if the website or its data practices change. The date
above will reflect the latest published revision.
